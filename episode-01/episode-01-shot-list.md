# The Rich Lion Who Underestimated His Daughter

Format: Vertical 9:16, 3D animated, Hindi/Hinglish dialogue
Runtime: about 3 minutes (34 shots). Trim shot 22 or 29 if you need it shorter.

## Character and style lock

Paste this block into every shot prompt so the characters stay consistent.

**Style:** High-quality 3D cartoon animation, Pixar-style anthropomorphic animals, cinematic lighting, expressive faces, vertical 9:16 framing.

**Raja Sher Singh (father):** Gigantic, muscular white lion billionaire. White suit, gold chains, expensive gold watch. Arrogant posture, smug expression.

**Rocky (son):** Spoiled young lion. Gold jacket, sunglasses. Confident, foolish, loud.

**Rani (daughter):** Clever young lioness. Simple cotton clothes, no jewellery. Observant, calm, focused.

**Chintu (rat):** Tiny, scruffy, brown rat. Sharp observant eyes. Stays scruffy throughout, even after winning.

**White tiger cub:** Huge, white fur, blue eyes, golden collar.

**Crowd, servants, announcer:** Background animals in an upscale stadium setting.
